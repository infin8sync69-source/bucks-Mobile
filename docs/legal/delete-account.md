# Delete your Bucks account

> **Draft for legal review.** Last updated: 9 November 2026. The contact email on this page is a placeholder until legal review is complete.

**In the app:** Profile → ⚙ Settings → **Delete account** → confirm.

**Without the app:** email support@bucks.example from any address, with your registered mobile number. We'll confirm by a one-time code to that number and delete the account within 7 days.

**What's deleted:** your profile, phone number, date of birth, guardian consent, posts, Moments, comments, votes, saved addresses, feedback posts, syncs, blocks, settings and sign-in. Messages you sent show as "Message deleted". Files you uploaded are erased within 30 days.

**What's kept:** reports about content you posted, as moderation records for 180 days (IT Rules, 2021), and records of completed rides and orders where tax law requires, with your name and contact details removed. Nothing else.
