# Community guidelines

> Last updated: 9 November 2026.

Bucks is for you and the people close to you. Keep it a place people want to be.

**Not allowed**
- Bullying, harassment, threats, or sharing someone's private information
- Hate speech: attacks on people for religion, caste, race, gender, sexuality, disability or where they're from
- Nudity or sexual content, and **anything sexual involving anyone under 18. We report this to the authorities.**
- Violence, or encouraging self-harm or dangerous acts
- Scams, fraud, spam, fake accounts, or pretending to be someone else
- Selling drugs, weapons or anything illegal, or offering services you aren't licensed for
- Fake reviews or recommendations, or paying for them
- Contacting minors you don't know, or asking them to move to another app

**What happens**
Anyone can report a post, comment, Moment, message or account. Our team reviews reports, and the most serious ones (child safety, self-harm) come first. We may remove content, suspend accounts, or tell the police.

**If you or someone else is in danger:** call 112. Childline 1098. Tele-MANAS (mental health, free, 24×7) 14416. Cyber crime 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in.
