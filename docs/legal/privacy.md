# Privacy policy

> **Draft for legal review.** Last updated: [date]. Replace every `[bracketed]` item.

Bucks is a social app run by **[Company legal name]**, [registered address], India ("we"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

## What we collect

| Data | Why | Shared with |
|---|---|---|
| **Mobile number** | Sign-in by one-time code; one account per person | Firebase Authentication (Google) for the code. Never shown to other users |
| **Name, bio, area, Bucks ID** | Your profile | Other Bucks users |
| **Date of birth** | Sets your age band so under-18s get extra protections. Never shown on your profile | Nobody |
| **Parent or guardian's name and mobile number** (under 18 only) | Record of their consent | Nobody |
| **Posts, comments, Moments, photos and videos** | What you share, with the audience you choose | The people you share them with |
| **Messages and files in chats** | Delivering your conversations | The people in that chat |
| **Syncs, blocks, close friends, settings** | Running your network and privacy choices | Nobody (a sync is visible to the other person) |
| **Approximate location (about 2 km)** | Nearby posts and Moments, suggestions | Not shown to anyone. Your exact location isn't stored |
| **Phone contacts** | Shows which of your contacts are on Bucks | Read on your phone only. **Not uploaded** |
| **Reports you make** | Safety review | Our moderation team. The person reported isn't told who reported them |
| **Feedback board posts** | Improving Bucks | Other Bucks users (under-18s shown as "Bucks member") |
| **Push notification token** | Sending you notifications | Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) |
| **Crash reports** | Fixing bugs. No user identifier | Firebase Crashlytics (Google) |
| **Usage statistics** (adults only, and you can turn it off) | Seeing which features are used | Google Analytics for Firebase. No advertising ID, no user ID, never for anyone under 18 |

We don't sell personal data, show ads or use an advertising ID.

**Where it's stored:** Supabase (database and files) and Google Firebase (sign-in, notifications, crash reports), on servers that may be outside India. [Confirm regions.]

## Children and teens

- Under 13: [not allowed in version 1.0 / allowed only with a parent-managed account from version 1.1].
- 13 to 17: a parent or legal guardian must consent before the account can be used. Teen accounts are private by default, only syncs can message them, adults they aren't connected to can't find them, their location is blurred, and we never track them for analytics or target them with advertising.

## How long we keep it

- Until you delete your account. Deletion removes your profile, phone number, date of birth, posts, Moments, comments, messages (shown to others as "Message deleted"), feedback posts and settings straight away, and the files you uploaded within [30] days.
- Reports about content stay as moderation records for [180 days / as the IT Rules require].
- Crash reports: 90 days. Usage statistics: [14 months] (Google's setting).

## Your rights

You can access, correct or erase your data, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to. Most of this is in the app (Edit profile, Settings, Delete account). For anything else, email [support email] or our grievance officer (see [Grievance](grievance)). You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
