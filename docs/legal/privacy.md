# Privacy policy

> **Draft for legal review.** Last updated: 9 November 2026. The company name, address, contacts and officer names on this page are placeholders until legal review is complete.

Bucks is an app for people, local services and shops near you, run by **Bucks Technologies Private Limited**, 2nd Floor, 12th Main Road, Indiranagar, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560038, India ("we"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

## What we collect

### Your account and social activity

| Data | Why | Shared with |
|---|---|---|
| **Mobile number** | Sign-in by one-time code; one account per person | Firebase Authentication (Google) for the code. Never shown to other users |
| **Name, bio, area, profile and cover photo, Bucks ID** | Your profile | Other Bucks users |
| **Date of birth** | Sets your age band so under-18s get extra protections. Never shown on your profile | Nobody |
| **Parent or guardian's name and mobile number** (under 18 only) | Record of their consent | Nobody |
| **Posts, comments, Moments, photos and videos** | What you share, with the audience you choose. Location data is removed from videos when you upload them | The people you share them with |
| **Messages and files in chats** | Delivering your conversations | The people in that chat |
| **Syncs, blocks, close friends, settings** | Running your network and privacy choices | Nobody (a sync is visible to the other person) |
| **Recommendations and reviews you give** | Ranking local shops and people by real recommendations | Other Bucks users see the counts; the business sees who recommended it |
| **Approximate location (about 2 km)** | Nearby posts, Moments and services, suggestions | Not shown to anyone. Your exact location isn't stored for posts |
| **Phone contacts** | Shows which of your contacts are on Bucks, and lets you call, message or invite them | Your phonebook is read on your phone and **not uploaded** |
| **Contact details you link to a Bucks person** (only if you choose to: their phone numbers, emails, organisation, address, a note) | Keeping what you know about someone together with their Bucks profile | Saved to your account and visible only to you. Deleted with your account |
| **Reports you make** | Safety review | Our moderation team. The person reported isn't told who reported them |
| **Feedback board posts** | Improving Bucks | Other Bucks users (under-18s shown as "Bucks member") |

### Rides, orders and services (when you use them)

| Data | Why | Shared with |
|---|---|---|
| **Precise location** (only while you book or are on a ride or delivery) | Setting your pickup point and showing the trip on the map | The driver or rider who takes it, during that trip |
| **Pickup and drop points, trip details and fares** | Booking and completing a ride or delivery | The driver or rider who takes it |
| **Delivery and saved addresses** (name, mobile number, address, pincode) | Delivering your orders | The shop and the rider or courier delivering that order |
| **Orders, carts and order history** | Placing, tracking and rating orders | The shop you ordered from |
| **Service requests and job applications** (including a CV you choose to attach) | Contacting a professional or applying for a job | The professional or employer you send them to |

### If you offer services on Bucks (drivers, shops, professionals)

| Data | Why | Shared with |
|---|---|---|
| **Business or skill profile, catalogue, prices, photos** | Your public page | Everyone who finds your page |
| **Vehicle details and documents** (registration certificate, insurance, driving licence, permit) and **business documents** (for example an FSSAI licence) | Checking that you can legally offer the service | Bucks staff who review them. Not shown publicly |
| **Your precise location while you are online as a driver** (a notification shows the whole time; it stops when you go offline) | Offering you nearby ride and delivery requests, and checking arrival and drop | The customer of a trip you accept, during that trip |
| **UPI payment QR** | Showing customers how to pay you | Your customers |

Bucks does not process card or UPI payments itself: payments go directly from the customer's UPI app to yours, or are paid in cash.

### Technical data

| Data | Why | Shared with |
|---|---|---|
| **Push notification token** | Sending you notifications | Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) |
| **Crash reports** | Fixing bugs. No user identifier | Firebase Crashlytics (Google) |
| **Usage statistics** (adults only, and you can turn it off) | Seeing which features are used | Google Analytics for Firebase. No advertising ID, no user ID, never for anyone under 18 |
| **Voice search** (only when you tap the microphone) | Turning what you say into a search | Your phone's speech service (for example Google) converts it to text. Bucks receives only the text, never the audio |
| **Map and place searches, routes** | Showing maps, finding places and working out routes | Mapbox; and, when Mapbox has no answer, OpenStreetMap services (Photon by Komoot, Nominatim, OSRM). They receive the search text or the points of the route, not your identity |
| **Pincode lookups** | Filling in city and state when you type a pincode | api.postalpincode.in receives the pincode only |

We don't sell personal data, show ads or use an advertising ID.

**Where it's stored:** our database and uploaded files are with Supabase in Mumbai, India (AWS ap-south-1). Sign-in, notifications, crash reports and usage statistics are handled by Google Firebase, whose servers may be outside India.

## Children and teens

- Under 13: not allowed in version 1.0.
- 13 to 17: a parent or legal guardian must consent before the account can be used. Teen accounts are private by default, only syncs can message them, adults they aren't connected to can't find them, their location is blurred, and we never track them for analytics or target them with advertising.

## How long we keep it

- Until you delete your account. Deletion removes your profile, phone number, date of birth, posts, Moments, comments, messages (shown to others as "Message deleted"), saved addresses, feedback posts and settings straight away, and the files you uploaded within 30 days.
- Records of completed rides and orders are kept for 8 years where tax and accounting law requires, with your name and contact details removed.
- Reports about content stay as moderation records for 180 days, or longer if the IT Rules, 2021 or a legal order require it.
- Crash reports: 90 days. Usage statistics: 14 months (Google's setting).

## Your rights

You can access, correct or erase your data, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to. Most of this is in the app (Edit profile, Settings, Delete account). For anything else, email support@bucks.example or our grievance officer (see [Grievance](grievance)). You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
