# Child safety standards

> **Draft for legal review.** Last updated: 9 November 2026. The contact on this page is a placeholder until legal review is complete.

Bucks has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming and sextortion.

**Prevention**
- Date of birth at sign-up. Under-18 accounts need a parent or guardian's consent.
- Teen accounts are private by default. Only people they've synced with can message them. Adults they share no connection with can't find them in search or suggestions. Their location is never stored precisely.
- Under-13s are not allowed in version 1.0.

**Reporting:** every post, comment, Moment, message and profile has **Report**, including the reason "A child may be at risk". These reports go to the top of our moderation queue.

**Action:** we remove CSAE content, suspend the accounts involved, preserve evidence, and report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and Indian law enforcement (cybercrime.gov.in), as the law requires.
