# Haram keyword classification (India): gambling, sorcery, riba, illegal goods

Tag legend (as requested):
- WORD = whole-word match on Latin text (case-insensitive). A multi-word entry means "all these words, adjacent, each whole-word" (a phrase).
- EXACT = whole word only, no suffixes (used for short or risky tokens, any script).
- PREFIX = Indic-script stem that takes case/plural suffixes (match at word start).

Source note: the fiqh and Indian-law findings below are sourced. The native-script spellings and most romanised ad spellings come from the researcher's own knowledge of Hindi, Urdu, Kannada, Malayalam, Tamil and Telugu and of Indian ad wording. They were NOT checked against a dictionary in this session (see Gaps). A native speaker should review the Indic columns before shipping.

## Q1. Gambling, lottery and betting: Indian forms and ad wording

### Takeaway
Paid-entry lotteries, prize draws and all betting are gambling (maysir/qimar) in mainstream Sunni and Shia rulings, and charity use of the money does not make it lawful. Indian law adds weight: satta matka is illegal everywhere, and the Online Gaming Act 2025 bans real-money online games, including rummy and fantasy sports. The risk is false positives (matka, satta, rummy, poker, bumper), so most entries must be phrases, not single words.

### Cited Findings
- Draws and lotteries are gambling because the participant pays money for something of no tangible value, and the outcome is win or lose. — [IslamQA 98151](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/98151)
- Contests that require paying to enter are gambling. A prize draw with free entry is permissible. — [IslamQA 20993](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/20993)
- Scholars differ on shop prize draws. Some call them haram, others permit them if prices are not raised to fund the prize. This makes "lucky draw" doubtful, not clear. — [IslamQA 97648](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/97648)
- Spending lottery profits on charity does not make the lottery permissible. — [aliftaa.jo (Jordan Ifta) on charity lotteries](https://aliftaa.jo/en-fatwas/4000/rss.aspx)
- Shia view (Sistani): chess and backgammon are forbidden with or without betting. Games commonly known as gambling games (dominoes, billiards, card games) are to be avoided even without betting, as an obligatory precaution. Other games with no betting are permissible. — [sistani.org Q&A 01161](https://www.sistani.org/english/qa/01161); [sistani.org gambling Q&A](https://www.sistani.org/english/qa/15234/). Other Shia jurists (Khomeini, Khwansari) tied the ban on chess to betting. — [WikiShia: Chess](https://en.wikishia.net/view/Chess)
- Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act 2025 (assent 22 Aug 2025) bans all online money games, whether of skill or chance. Commentators note it treats rummy and fantasy cricket like roulette. Penalties reach operators, advertisers and banks. — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/08/india-moves-to-outlaw-real-money-gaming-with-sweeping-ban); [Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/law-firms/view-point/legal-gamechanger-what-the-online-gaming-act-2025-means-for-indias-gaming-industry); [Mondaq](https://webiis08.mondaq.com/india/gaming/1677248/online-gaming-act-2025-game-over-for-rmg). The Act also recognises e-sports and online social games. — same sources
- Satta matka: Kalyan Matka network operators and bookies arrested in Goa and Mumbai raids (May 2025); a "Satta King" asset attachment by ED in MP (Feb 2025); Pune "Matka King" den raided. — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/mumbais-matka-queen-jaya-chheda-arrested-in-goa-gambling-crackdown); [Dainik Jagran MP](https://english.dainikjagranmpcg.com/states/madhya-pradesh/gambling-racket-busted-in-mhow-18-arrested-as-mp-police/article-15180); [Bridge Chronicle](https://www.thebridgechronicle.com/news/pune-matka-king-nandu-naiks-gambling-den-in-shukrawar-peth-raided). SEO pages advertise "Kolkata Fatafat", "Gujarat FF" and "Kalyan matka chart" results. — [LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/india/information/kolkata-fatafat-result-gujarat-ff-live-kalyan-matka-chart-financial-risks-and-legal-consequences-of-illegal-lotteries-7330936.html)
- State lotteries are legal only where the state runs them, under the Lotteries (Regulation) Act 1998. A Lok Sabha-based list for 2023 names Arunachal, Goa, Kerala, Maharashtra, Mizoram, Nagaland, Punjab, Sikkim and West Bengal. Kerala banned online and single-digit lotteries (upheld 2015). Illegal Kerala-lottery smuggling persists. — [PW OnlyIAS](https://pwonlyias.com/current-affairs/lotteries-in-india-regulation-gst-constitutional-framework/); [righttoinformation.wiki](https://righttoinformation.wiki/is-online-lottery-legal-india-2026); [SiGMA](https://sigma.world/news/illegal-kerala-lottery-smuggling-persists-in-india/). Note: legal under Indian law does not make it halal; per the IslamQA rulings above, a state lottery is still gambling.

### Inferences
- A state lottery being legal in Kerala or Nagaland does not change the ruling. Lottery tickets, agents, results and "bumper" draws are clear-haram listings.
- Fantasy sports and online rummy for money are now both gambling in fiqh and illegal under PROGA 2025. The caller's list puts "fantasy sports" under doubtful. Recommendation: keep the bare words "fantasy", "dream11" and "rummy" doubtful (free-to-play, card sets and team-kit merchandise exist). Hide the money phrases instead ("fantasy cash", "rummy cash", "real money game").
- Card games such as teen patti and andar bahar are, in Indian use, almost always played for money. Sistani counts card games as gambling games even without a bet. Hide them.

## Q2. Sorcery / black magic: Indian ad wording (ruqyah allowed)

### Takeaway
Ads for vashikaran, kala jadu, "love problem solution baba" and regional equivalents (koodothram, seivinai, chetabadi, banamati, maata) are clear sihr. Several states also outlaw them: Karnataka's 2017 Act bans advertising such practices, and the Drugs and Magic Remedies Act 1954 bans ads for magic remedies. Ruqyah that uses Qur'an, dhikr or understood du'a, with no shirk, is permissible by scholarly consensus. Never hide "ruqyah", "ruqya" or "dam" alone.

### Cited Findings
- Karnataka Prevention and Eradication of Inhuman Evil Practices and Black Magic Act 2017: Section 3 bars committing, promoting or practising scheduled acts, including advertising or propagating them. The schedule covers "black magic in search of precious things, bounty and hidden treasures" and creating an impression of possession and exorcism. In force from 4 Jan 2020. Sources disagree on the count of banned practices (16 vs 23) and on penalties. — [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/7807); [The Better India](https://www.thebetterindia.com/121175/karnataka-anti-superstition-bill/); [Onmanorama](https://www.onmanorama.com/news/india/2020/01/23/anti-superstition-bill-notified-in-karnataka.html); [The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/explainers/all-you-need-to-know-about-karnatakas-anti-superstition-bill); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/3-yrs-after-passage-bjp-govt-notifies-superstition-law-797238.html)
- The Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act 1954 bans magic-remedy ads by anyone professing magic. A court petition cites tantrik ads promising solutions for "service, business, love, marriage, vashikaran, childlessness". — [India Code PDF](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/bitstream/123456789/1412/1/A1954-21.pdf); [Indian Kanoon petition](https://future.indiankanoon.org/doc/190898482/)
- Kerala: police describe "Koodothram" as black magic (arrest of "Koodothram Sivan", Palakkad). Kottayam 2025: a sorcerer was arrested over a ritual to "evict a ghost". There were 2022 "human sacrifice" killings in Pathanamthitta. — [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/amp/story/kerala/kerala-cops-arrest-man-who-posed-temple-priest-steal-gold-money-devotees-112180); [Onmanorama 2025](https://www.onmanorama.com/news/kerala/2025/11/08/black-magic-ritual-woman-tortured.html); [Onmanorama 2022](https://www.onmanorama.com/news/kerala/2022/10/11/black-magic-deaths-kochi-serial-killer.html)
- Ruqyah is permissible by consensus when it uses Allah's words, names or attributes, is in Arabic or an understood language, and is believed to work only by Allah's will. A ruqyah containing shirk or unknown phrases is not allowed. — [IslamQA 7874](https://islamqa.info/en/ref/7874); [IslamQA 209745](https://islamqa.info/en/downloads/answers/209745); [Jordan Ifta via islamqa.org](https://islamqa.org/shafii/darul-iftaa-jordan/229002/doing-ruqyia-with-words-of-remembrance-or-words-that-dont-contradict-shariah-is-acceptable/)
- Astrology: IslamQA says astrology (as opposed to astronomy) is unlawful, and believing the stars cause events is kufr. Islamweb treats astrologers as fortune-tellers (kahin) and says a Muslim should not read horoscope columns. A Shia source (al-islam.org) says foretelling a person's future is not permissible and the money earned is unlawful. — [IslamQA 22445](https://islamqa.info/en/22445); [Islamweb](https://islamweb.net/en/women/article/88495/islam-and-fortune-telling); [al-islam.org](https://al-islam.org/node/127622). **This conflicts with the caller's plan to treat astrology as doubtful.** See Inferences.

### Inferences
- **Flag for the coordinator:** the sources treat fortune-telling astrology as haram in both Sunni and Shia rulings, not as doubtful. It stays on the DOUBTFUL list here only because the caller instructed it, and because Indian "astrology" listings mostly cover jathakam/kundli matching for Hindu weddings, panchang calendars and gemstone sales, all culturally embedded and mixed. The product owner should decide this explicitly. A middle path: hide only "future prediction baba" or "love problem astrologer" style phrases (already covered below as sorcery-ads), and leave plain "astrology" visible.
- "Jinn removal", "nazar utarna", "taweez", "amil" and "dam" may describe permissible ruqyah or forbidden practice. Leave them visible. Hide only the clearly sihr wording ("jinn hazir", "hamzad", "sifli amal").
- Many Indic sorcery words have a second, innocent sense: vasiyam/vasheekaranam (charm/captivate), maata (shape/physique), eval (errand), bandish (restriction/musical composition), sihr/sahar (dawn). Use phrases or EXACT for these.

## Q3. Riba: is a moneylender-at-interest listing clearly haram to show? Which wording is unambiguous?

### Takeaway
Yes. Advertising a private loan at interest is a listing of a riba service, and Deobandi rulings treat both giving and taking interest as haram. The unambiguous wording is "interest word + lend/money" in the regional idiom (vatti-ku panam, byaj par paisa, baddi saala, vaddi ki dabbulu, palisha-kku panam) and the named usury forms (meter vatti, kandhu vatti, daily vatti, thandal, blade company). Bank loans, EMI, gold loans, insurance, chit funds and "0% interest" offers are doubtful. Leave them visible.

### Cited Findings
- Darul Uloom Deoband Darul Ifta: dealing in riba without severe need is a major sin. A bank loan for business is "not allowed; because this involves giving interest, and both giving and taking interest is haram". Interest is haram for Muslims everywhere, including in non-Muslim countries. — [islamqa.org (Deoband) 27943](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/darulifta-deoband/27943/is-a-bank-loan-permissible-in-india-for-a-business-purpose/); [islamqa.org (Deoband) 23608](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/darulifta-deoband/23608/is-paying-interest-allowed-in-islam/); [Darul Ifta Deoband FAQ: Usury/Insurance](https://darulifta.dud.edu.in/faq/114)
- Deoband also allowed mosque-held bank interest to be given to the needy (Covid lockdown). The Muslim Personal Law Board condemned its 2018 fatwa against marrying into bank-employee families. This shows that ordinary banking touches many Indian Muslims and that rulings on it are contested in practice. — [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.in/india/news/darul-uloom-allows-use-of-accumulated-bank-interests-of-mosque-to-help-needy-amid-lockdown/amp_articleshow/75553768.cms); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/article/news-ani/muslims-divided-over-fatwa-against-marrying-bankers-118010500221_1.html)
- Tamil Nadu Prohibition of Charging Exorbitant Interest Act 2003 names "daily vatti, hourly vatti, kandhu vatti, meter vatti and thandal" as exorbitant interest forms. Meter vatti can reach about 10% per day. Chennai police set up a "kandhuvatti" wing (2014). — [TN Act PDF (cra.tn.gov.in)](https://www.cra.tn.gov.in/tnscs/Files/act/012_The%20Tamil%20Nadu%20Prohibition%20of%20Charging%20Exorbitant%20Interest%20Act%202003.pdf); [Scroll](https://scroll.in/article/855716/tamil-nadu-has-stringent-laws-against-moneylending-at-exorbitant-rates-why-does-it-persist-then)

### Inferences
- Showing a moneylender listing helps someone find riba. The well-known hadith of Jabir in Sahih Muslim curses the one who takes riba, the one who gives it, its scribe and its two witnesses. Qur'an 5:2 forbids cooperating in sin. Both support hiding such listings. (The hadith and verse are standard references. They were not fetched in this session.)
- Bank and NBFC products are riba-based too, but the caller classes them as doubtful and they are widely used under necessity rulings. Keep them visible. Hide only peer-to-peer or private "money on interest" ads.
- "Blade" for usurious lenders is Kerala slang ("blade company", "blade mafia"), from researcher knowledge. Hide only the phrase "blade company/blade palisha", never "blade" alone.

## Q4. Clearly illegal goods in India (wildlife, weapons, explosives, counterfeit, organs, scams)

### Takeaway
Unambiguous item names: hatha jodi, pangolin scales, ivory/hathi daant (as material, not colour), tiger skin/claw/tooth, deer skin (mrigchala), shahtoosh, mongoose-hair brush, siyar singhi; desi katta/tamancha/country-made pistol, live cartridges; gelatin sticks, detonators; fake currency/FICN; fake Aadhaar/PAN/degree/marksheet; kidney for sale; rice puller, copper iridium, red mercury, money doubling. Each needs a phrase or a material context, because the bare words (ivory, tiger, katta, bullet, kidney, fake, duplicate) are common false positives.

### Cited Findings
- Hatha jodi is the hemipenis of the monitor lizard (Varanus spp., Schedule I, Wild Life Protection Act 1972). It is sold online (Flipkart/Amazon), with seizures of 120 pieces and 6 arrests, and 38 pieces seized by WCCB in Ajmer (Sep 2024). — [Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/amp/story/wildlife-biodiversity/traders-arrested-for-selling-illegal-wildlife-parts-like-hatha-jodi-on-flipkart-amazon); [WCS India Sep 2024](https://india.wcs.org/Newsroom/News/ID/23728/Illegal-Wildlife-Trade-News-September-2024)
- Pangolin scales were seized by WCCB in Assam, Bihar and MP (2024-25). Elephant ivory was seized with pangolin scales in Assam (Feb 2025). Tiger and leopard skins, claws and teeth were seized across states. — [WCS India Oct 2024](https://india.wcs.org/Newsroom/News/ID/23815/Illegal-Wildlife-Trade-News-October-2024); [WCS India Jan 2025](https://india.wcs.org/Newsroom/News/ID/24136/Illegal-Wildlife-Trade-Newsletter-January-2025); [WCS India Feb 2025](https://india.wcs.org/Newsroom/News/ID/24261/Illegal-Wildlife-Trade-Newsletter-February-2025); [WTI](https://www.wti.org.in/news/the-dark-underbelly-of-amritsar-from-sacred-city-to-wildlife-smuggling-hub/)

Cases are booked under Arms Act ss. 25, 54, 59. — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/madhya-pradesh/facebook-page-advertises-pistols-for-sale-with-promise-of-home-delivery-2892161); [Bridge Chronicle](https://www.thebridgechronicle.com/pune/pune-crime-branch-arrests-instagram-based-pistol-dealer-latur-agn97); [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/karnataka-illegal-pistol-racket-key-accused-held-in-hyderabad-3551957/); [Tribune "desi katta cartridge"](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/jalandhar/police-arrest-man-with-desi-katta-cartridge)

Courts allow altruistic donor appeals that offer no payment. — [India Code THOA](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/19260/1/thoa_act_1994.pdf); [lawx.in s.19](https://lawx.in/acts/section/78/680/6404); [Indian Kanoon](https://future.indiankanoon.org/doc/118747797/)

### Inferences
- Fraud and selling unlawful items are haram (cheating, harming others, breaking lawful agreements). The illegal-goods list is therefore treated as clear-haram for listings. Selling a weapon in itself is not haram in fiqh. Illegal unlicensed arms are hidden because the sale is a crime, which is why licensed-dealer words like "gun shop" and "rifle club" stay doubtful.
- "Red mercury" did not appear in the Indian news results fetched here. Researcher knowledge says it is a long-running scam item in India and the Gulf. Include it, at slightly lower confidence.
- Kerala "panni padakkam" (pig-bait explosive) and Tamil "naattu vedi/naattu bomb" (country bomb) are from researcher knowledge, not fetched. "vedi" alone means firecracker. Hide only the phrases.
- Karnataka's 2023 tiger-claw pendant arrests (ಹುಲಿ ಉಗುರು, "huli uguru") are from researcher knowledge, not fetched.
- Commercial surrogacy (Surrogacy Regulation Act 2021) and paid blood donation bans are relevant but were not researched here. Treat them as gaps.
